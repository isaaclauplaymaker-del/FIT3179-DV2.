Who needs more support?

For Malaysian readers interested in Australian school education.

Why these skills matter

Literacy helps students understand information, evaluate ideas and communicate across school subjects. Numeracy helps them apply mathematical knowledge to quantities, measurements and data in everyday situations. Identifying gaps in these skills helps teachers plan the next steps in learning.

This story uses Reading as one component of literacy; it does not measure every literacy skill. Its question is where additional support may be needed, rather than what causes the differences.

NAPLAN, the National Assessment Program – Literacy and Numeracy, assesses students in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. “Needs additional support” is an official proficiency category. “Strong or Exceeding” combines the two highest categories. Standards differ by subject and year level.

Support needs have more than one dimension

National results, geographic differences, participation and student-group profiles offer different views of literacy and numeracy performance. Interpret them together, while remembering that a group result does not predict an individual student's achievement.
