EDUCATION IN AUSTRALIA · NAPLAN 2026

Literacy and Numeracy in Australia

Who needs more support?

National averages provide a starting point. Reading and numeracy outcomes also vary across year levels, places and student backgrounds.

NAPLAN is the National Assessment Program – Literacy and Numeracy. It assesses students in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. This story examines Reading and Numeracy.

“Needs additional support” is an official proficiency category. “Strong or Exceeding” combines the two highest categories. Standards are specific to each subject and year level.

Support needs have more than one dimension

National results, geographic differences, participation and student-group profiles offer different views of literacy and numeracy performance. Interpret them together, while remembering that a group result does not predict an individual student's achievement.
