WHY LOOK AT THESE SKILLS?

Understanding information.
Working with numbers.

Literacy helps people understand and communicate information. Numeracy helps them use quantities, measurements and data in everyday situations. Both support learning across school subjects.

This story uses Reading as one part of literacy. It asks where reported support needs differ; it does not establish what causes those differences.

What is NAPLAN, and what do the categories mean?

NAPLAN is Australia’s National Assessment Program – Literacy and Numeracy, taken in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. These are Australian school year levels, not student ages. The four proficiency categories are Needs additional support, Developing, Strong and Exceeding. Exempt students are reported separately. This page combines Strong and Exceeding in some comparisons; standards differ by subject and year level.

ABOUT THIS VISUALISATION

The purpose behind
the comparisons

Designed for general readers in Malaysia interested in Australian school education. No knowledge of coding or NAPLAN is assumed.

Why these comparisons?

The aim is to help readers identify differences in reported reading and numeracy support needs across Australian places, student backgrounds and assessment years. National results provide the starting point; group comparisons reveal differences within that overall picture.

What data is shown?

ACARA NAPLAN results cover 2023–2026, Years 3, 5, 7 and 9, and Reading and Numeracy. ABS school enrolments provide separate 2025 population context. Reading is one component of literacy, and the two sources describe different student populations.

How do the charts help?

Heatmaps and proficiency bars compare percentages; maps locate geographic differences; profile and trend charts compare groups and years. The choropleth uses colour for support shares, the participant map uses circle area for counts, and the enrolment cartogram uses state-shape area for population size. Filters change the assessment, while state highlighting connects related views.

READ THE RESULTS WITH CARE

Understanding
the comparisons

One part of literacy

Reading results do not cover every literacy skill. Proficiency standards are specific to the subject and year level.

Different annual cohorts

Changes over time compare different students at the same year level. They do not track individual progress.

Missing does not mean zero

Suppressed achievement remains missing. Tasmania’s 2026 achievement is not reported because of low participation; its participation data remains visible.

Different populations

2025 ABS enrolments cover all school year levels. NAPLAN counts cover the selected assessment. Group differences do not establish their causes.
